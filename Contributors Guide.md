# Nigeria AI Collective Repository — Contributor Guide

Welcome to the **Nigeria AI Collective Repository**, our community initiative to document, promote, and connect the entire AI development landscape in Nigeria.

Your contributions are vital! They help showcase the creativity, innovation, and research leadership emerging from Nigeria’s growing AI ecosystem.

Thank you for helping make AI knowledge open, inclusive, and accessible to all.

---

## 1. Ways to Contribute

You can contribute to this repository in the following key areas:

| Type | Description | Example |
| :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Research Contributions** | Add peer-reviewed papers, preprints, whitepapers, or technical reports either authored by Nigerians or focused on AI in Nigeria. | “Evaluating Machine Translation for African Languages” (ICLR 2025) |
| **Startup / Organization** | Add verified Nigerian AI startups, innovation hubs, or research centers. | EqualyzAI, Awarri, NCAIR, NITDA-AI Collective |
| **Open-Source & Datasets** | Provide links to datasets, APIs, or AI tools developed by Nigerian researchers or teams. | Igbo API, AfroSLM, MasakhaNER 2.0 |
| **Events & Hackathons** | Document ongoing or past events that strengthen the Nigerian AI community. | Zindi Africa Challenges, DSN Bootcamp, NCAIR Hackathon |
| **Awards & Recognition** | Highlight individuals or teams recognized for excellence in AI research, ethics, or innovation. | AI4Good Nigeria Awards 2025 |

## 2. How to Submit Your Contribution

Follow these five simple steps to submit your entry:

1.  **Fork the Repository**
    Click the **Fork** button at the top right of this page.

2.  **Create a New Branch**

    ```bash
    git checkout -b add-your-name-or-project
    ```

3.  **Edit the Relevant Section**
    Add your entry to the correct section of the `README` (e.g., “AI Startups” or “Datasets”). Please use the example formats below to ensure consistency.

4.  **Commit and Push**

    ```bash
    git commit -m "Added [brief description of your contribution]"
    git push origin add-your-name-or-project
    ```

5.  **Submit a Pull Request (PR)**
    Go to your fork, click **“Compare & Pull Request,”** and provide a brief explanation of what you added and why it's relevant.

## 5. Contribution Principles

Before submitting, please ensure your entry adheres to these principles:

* **Is accurate and verifiable** (use official or trusted links).
* **Fits the repository’s structure and markdown format.**
* **Reflects AI-related activity** in or connected to Nigeria.
* **Respects the Code of Conduct** (be kind, professional, and collaborative).
* **Avoids promotional, duplicate, or off-topic content.**

## 6. Code of Conduct
